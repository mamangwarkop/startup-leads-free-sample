# Free Startup Leads (updated from live launches)

40 freshly launched startups from Hacker News "Show HN", enriched with **contact emails, X/LinkedIn, ad-pixel audit, lead score and an outreach hook**.

**Download:** [`sample_leads.csv`](sample_leads.csv) (free, MIT) - about 40% of rows have a verified contact email.

## Columns
`name, tagline, maker, website, emails, twitter, linkedin, pixels, missing, score, pitch, hn, date`

- `missing` = marketing pixels NOT installed (Meta / GA / TikTok) = your pitch angle
- `score` = 0-100 outreach readiness
